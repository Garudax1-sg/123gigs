Frequently asked questions

How 123Gigs works.

What is 123Gigs?

123Gigs is an on-demand workforce marketplace where customers post tasks, set their own budgets and connect with people willing to take on the work.

What kind of jobs can businesses post?

Almost any legitimate task that can be clearly described — from shelf stacking, packing and stocktaking to event support, cleaning, painting, moving, merchandising, admin and creative work.

Does 123Gigs set the price?

No. The customer sets the budget for the gig. Workers can decide whether they want to take the task at that price.

Is 123Gigs a ride-hailing service?

No. 123Gigs is not a ride-hailing platform and does not provide passenger transport services.

Can businesses use 123Gigs for recurring work?

Yes. The marketplace can be used for one-off tasks as well as recurring or repeat work, subject to the platform's rules.

Is 123Gigs available in Singapore?

Yes. Singapore is the first 123Gigs market and the platform is currently live.
